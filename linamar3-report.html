Co-op Work Term Report

Business Analyst Co-op (3rd Term) | Linamar Corporation

Location: Guelph, Ontario | Term Length: 4 Months

Introduction

This website documents my third co-op work term as a Business Analyst at Linamar Corporation. Returning to the organization for a subsequent term allowed me to build upon the knowledge and experience gained during my previous placement while taking on more responsibility and ownership of projects that supported business operations.

Throughout this work term, I worked extensively with reporting, business intelligence, process documentation, and data analysis. I contributed to initiatives focused on improving reporting efficiency, increasing data visibility, and supporting decision-making across multiple business functions.

About the Employer

Linamar Corporation is a global advanced manufacturing company operating in mobility, infrastructure, and industrial solution sectors. Headquartered in Guelph, Ontario, Linamar relies heavily on integrated data systems to optimize global operations, supply chain management, and financial reporting.

From a computing and business intelligence perspective, Linamar emphasizes robust data governance, automated reporting workflows, and enterprise analytics platforms. Returning to this environment provided an expanded opportunity to leverage SQL, Power BI, and data infrastructure to solve real-world operational challenges.

Work Term Goals

At the beginning of this work term, I established the following goals:

  • Take greater ownership of reporting and analytical projects
  • Further develop advanced Power BI and data visualization skills
  • Improve efficiency through process improvements and automation opportunities
  • Strengthen stakeholder communication and requirement gathering abilities
  • Create detailed and sustainable documentation for future team use
  • Develop a deeper understanding of business operations and reporting systems
  • Deliver actionable insights that support business decision-making

Goal Reflection: As a returning co-op student, I was able to achieve these goals by contributing independently to ongoing initiatives and supporting the development of reporting solutions used by stakeholders. I gained greater confidence working with large datasets, managing requests, and presenting information in a clear and meaningful way.

The experience strengthened both my technical and professional skills while helping me understand how business intelligence tools support operational performance within a large manufacturing organization.

Job Description

During this work term, my responsibilities expanded beyond supporting existing reports and dashboards. I worked on enhancing reporting solutions, improving documentation, and collaborating with stakeholders to identify opportunities for process improvement.

  • Maintaining and enhancing Power BI dashboards and reports
  • Analyzing business and operational data using Excel, SQL, and reporting tools
  • Supporting data validation and quality assurance activities
  • Documenting business processes, workflows, and reporting logic
  • Gathering requirements from stakeholders and translating them into reporting solutions
  • Identifying opportunities to improve reporting efficiency and usability
  • Supporting continuous improvement initiatives through data-driven analysis

Because I was already familiar with the organization and its systems, I was able to become productive more quickly and focus on making meaningful contributions to projects rather than primarily learning foundational processes.

Key Learning and Reflection

One of the biggest lessons from this work term was learning how to balance technical analysis with business needs. Producing accurate reports is important, but understanding stakeholder requirements and delivering information in a useful format is equally critical.

I also gained valuable experience working more independently and taking ownership of deliverables. This helped strengthen my project management, problem-solving, and communication skills while increasing my confidence in professional environments.

Returning to Linamar provided an opportunity to see long-term business processes and understand how continuous improvements can generate meaningful value over time. This experience reinforced the importance of collaboration, documentation, and data-driven decision-making.

Conclusion

My third co-op work term at Linamar Corporation provided an excellent opportunity to deepen my business analysis and reporting experience. Building on the foundation established during my previous work term, I was able to contribute more independently while supporting projects that improved reporting, documentation, and business processes.

This experience further strengthened my interests in business intelligence, analytics, and technology-driven problem solving. The skills and knowledge gained during this term will continue to support my academic and professional development as I pursue a career in business and data analytics.

Acknowledgments

I would like to express my sincere gratitude to my manager, team members, and colleagues at Linamar Corporation for their continuous guidance and support throughout my third work term. Their ongoing mentorship and encouragement gave me the confidence to take ownership of projects and develop as a Business Analyst.
